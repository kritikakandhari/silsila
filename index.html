Our Story

Every flavour
carries a memory.

A familiar aroma from home. A meal shared with family. A street we once walked. A taste that takes us back to a moment we never really forgot.

SILSILA means “a continuation.” A continuation of the flavours we grew up with, the memories we carry, and the journey that brought us to Edmonton.

Beginning our story as a cloud kitchen, SILSILA is a dream taking its very first steps — created with the hope of bringing the warmth of home, the soul of India, and the excitement of something new straight to your table.

Because food has a beautiful way of connecting people across places, generations, and moments. And this is only the beginning of our SILSILA — a beautiful continuation of memories, flavours, dreams, people, and stories... one plate at a time.

Indianin soul
Modernin spirit
Madeto be remembered
— Vardaan & Sunakshita, founders

Some stories are told.
Ours are savoured — one plate at a time.
