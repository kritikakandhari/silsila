Frequently Asked Questions

What is Silsila?

Silsila is an Indian fusion cloud kitchen based in Edmonton, Alberta. We specialize in bringing bold, authentic Indian flavors into modern, global formats like bowls, wraps, and specialty beverages.

Do you offer dine-in seating?

No, we operate exclusively as a cloud kitchen. This means we focus 100% of our effort on preparing high-quality food for takeout and delivery, ensuring your meal travels perfectly from our kitchen to your table.

How can I place an order?

You can easily place an order for delivery through our partners on UberEats and DoorDash. Simply click the "Order Now" button at the top of the page to find our profiles on these platforms.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions?

Yes! We offer a variety of vegetarian and vegan options, such as our Veggie Garden and Tikka Paneer dishes. Please check our menu on UberEats or DoorDash for detailed ingredient information and allergy warnings.

What are your store hours?

Our store and kitchen hours are:
• Sunday & Monday: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM
• Tuesday & Wednesday: Closed
• Thursday: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM
• Friday & Saturday: 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Where are you located?

Our kitchen is located at 15355 111 Ave NW, Edmonton, Alberta, Canada T5P 0R7. Please note that this location is for order pickup and kitchen operations only, as we do not have a dine-in area.
